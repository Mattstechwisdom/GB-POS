# GadgetBoy POS v0.6.78

- Inventory re-up purchases now remain Incoming after supplier checkout instead of prematurely increasing On Hand stock.
- Marking an inventory-linked product or part delivered adds the ordered quantity to On Hand exactly once, with retry protection against duplicate stock.
- Inventory screens now distinguish On Hand quantities from Incoming quantities for clearer purchasing decisions.
- QR repair actions now drive explicit work-order stages, repair-queue routing, promises, part timing, pickup timing, technician activity, and Needs Attention reasons.
- Interactive client emails provide scoped approval, decline, question, information, pickup confirmation, and pickup-change controls without exposing the POS.
- Client Replies supports staff acknowledgment, email responses, resolution controls, right-click actions, and mobile long-press actions.
- Windows Auto Update uses Electron Updater's native handoff and an unattended per-user installer, with persistent updater diagnostics.
