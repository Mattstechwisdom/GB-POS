# GadgetBoy POS v0.6.59

This release completes the automatic repair email lifecycle.

- Diagnostic payment at check-in sends the branded device drop-off acknowledgment.
- Final labor payment that clears the remaining balance sends a separate repair-completion thank-you.
- The completion message identifies the device, repair, work order, and final labor amount.
- Ordered-part, diagnostic, and completion messages remain independently idempotent.
