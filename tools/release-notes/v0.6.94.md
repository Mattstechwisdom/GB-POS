# GadgetBoy POS v0.6.94

- Command Center Today entries now open the real task, event, note, consultation, work order, or sale.
- Closed and picked-up work orders remain closed when delayed synchronization returns an older active copy.
- Resolved client replies stay removed while Supabase finishes synchronizing their resolved state.
- Sales-ticket QR generation now retries temporary failures and will not silently print a sheet without its QR code.
- Includes the earlier v0.6.93 work-order QR timing fix so release forms wait for the QR image to render before printing.
