# GadgetBoy POS v0.6.80

- Adds Category Performance reporting for retail sales, repairs, and consultations.
- Adds dedicated beverage reporting with revenue, units sold, internal cost, gross profit, margin, current stock, incoming stock, low-stock warnings, and estimated stock coverage.
- Preserves inventory links and category snapshots on new sale lines while matching older sales by saved category and product description.
- Corrects Command Center task totals for date-only and recurring calendar tasks.
- Makes new work orders appear in the Command Center immediately after check-in.
- Keeps Active Work Orders, Checked In, Repair Queue, Awaiting Parts, and Ready for Pickup panels synchronized when tickets change or close elsewhere.
- Removes stale local repair-catalog rows after a successful cloud sync, collapses same-device duplicate services in Admin and the work-order picker, and updates an existing matching service instead of creating another copy.
- Adds an independent Windows relaunch guard that retries the silent installer handoff when an update closes the POS without installing or reopening it.
- Includes responsive category-report cards for phone and tablet layouts.
