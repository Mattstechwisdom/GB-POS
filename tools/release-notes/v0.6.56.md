# GadgetBoy POS v0.6.56

This release begins the customer-data integrity work on top of every change included through v0.6.55.

- Customer names, phone numbers, and email addresses now share one identity-normalization path.
- Exact contact matches, name-only suggestions, and conflicting records are classified separately so unsafe matches are not automatically merged.
- Canonical duplicate selection prioritizes records already linked to transactions, then completeness and age.
- Transaction customer labels preserve saved names and fall back to linked customer details before displaying a client number.
- Focused regression coverage protects the new identity rules.
