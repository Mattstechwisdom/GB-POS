## GadgetBoy POS v0.6.82

- Prevents closed work orders from reappearing when an older Supabase response arrives.
- Uses one shared create request across work-order autosave, Save, checkout, and receipt printing to prevent duplicate tickets and stale line items.
- Adds the correct repair QR to work-order receipts while preserving sale and consultation-specific QR routing.
- Corrects Today counts so consultations use their appointment date and are not counted as generic events.
- Makes each Today summary tile open a compact at-a-glance list and removes the duplicate lower Ready for Pickup panel.
- Keeps inventory restock delivery idempotent: stock is increased once, when an inventory-linked purchase is marked delivered.
- Keeps the public, token-scoped repair timeline free of POS login access and improves the empty-history and client-response wording.
