## GadgetBoy POS v0.6.65

This release brings the approved Command Center design into the production POS while preserving the existing shop workflows and record windows.

- Adds responsive size profiles for calendars, quote generation, inventory, repairs, reporting, and compact utility windows.
- Converts invoice, work-order, and sales tables into labeled cards on phones so every field remains readable without sideways scrolling.
- Makes the calendar use the available desktop space and fit the mobile viewport cleanly.
- Keeps controls readable and touch-friendly across desktop, tablet, portrait, and landscape layouts.
- Removes the redundant persistent notification panel while retaining the notification window behind the bell.
- Corrects active repair counts, technician-name resolution, and nested inventory variant presentation.

Existing checkout, client, work-order, sales, consultation, QR, email, inventory, repair, reporting, and Supabase synchronization behavior remains in place.
